David D.J. Roy is a Canadian stand-up comedian, storyteller, spoken word artist, actor, and booker. He came up in Vancouver — founding and hosting **That Filthy Show**, a long-time fixture at the Vancouver Poetry Slam — and turns personal history — adult-film work, homelessness, hitchhiking — into dark-and-light stage stories.

Clubs, hosting, slam, press, film, and one-man nights. Rooms that want safe and forgettable should book someone else.
