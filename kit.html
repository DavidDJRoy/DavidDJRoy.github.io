Short bio

David D.J. Roy is a Vancouver stand-up comedian, storyteller, spoken word artist, actor, and booker. He founded and hosted That Filthy Show, is a long-time fixture at the Vancouver Poetry Slam, and turns personal history — adult-film work, homelessness, hitchhiking — into dark-and-light stage stories. Independent film credits include No Joke (2013), The Vancouver B Movie Factory (2013), and Show Cop (2015).
