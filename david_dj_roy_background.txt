DAVID D.J. ROY - BACKGROUND INFORMATION & LINKS
===============================================

PROFESSIONAL BACKGROUND & BIO DETAILS
-------------------------------------
* Roles: Stand-up comedian, storyteller, spoken word artist, actor, and performer/choreographer. 
* Live Shows & Poetry: He has been a long-time fixture in the Vancouver arts scene. He frequently performed at the Vancouver Poetry Slam, presenting raw and acclaimed open-mic spoken word poems (notably "Homelessness", "Feminism?", and "Just a Job"). 
* "That Filthy Show": Founder and host of That Filthy Show, a comedy and variety show that embraced both dark and light storytelling.
* Vancouver International Burlesque Festival: Worked as a performer and choreographer.
* Adult Film Industry Past & Storytelling: Used to work in the adult film industry and turned that experience into a one-man, deeply engaging storytelling event called "Behind the Smut," where he bared all about his experiences and took audience questions. 
* Acting Credits: Acting roles in independent and short films, including "No Joke" (2013), "The Vancouver B Movie Factory" (2013), and "Show Cop" (2015).
* Themes: Known for discussing intense and personal topics openly—ranging from his time in the adult film industry, homelessness, and hitchhiking, to more lighthearted and dark storytelling.
