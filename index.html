About

Raw rooms. Real stories.

David D.J. Roy is a long-time fixture in the Vancouver arts scene: a stand-up comedian, storyteller, spoken word artist, actor, and performer/choreographer who also books and organizes comedy.

He has been a regular at the Vancouver Poetry Slam, delivering raw, acclaimed open-mic spoken word — including Homelessness, Feminism?, and Just a Job. He founded and hosted That Filthy Show, a comedy and variety night that made room for both dark and light storytelling.

As a performer and choreographer with the Vancouver International Burlesque Festival, and through Behind the Smut — a one-man storytelling event about his years in the adult film industry, with the floor open for questions — he has made a practice of talking about intense, personal material in public: adult work, homelessness, hitchhiking, and the jokes that survive them.

Independent and short-film credits include No Joke (2013), The Vancouver B Movie Factory (2013), and Show Cop (2015).
